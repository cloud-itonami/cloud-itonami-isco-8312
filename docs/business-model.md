# Business Model: Railway Brake, Signal and Switch Operator Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-8312`
- ISCO-08: `8312`
- Occupation: Railway Brake, Signal and Switch Operators
- Social impact: rail-safety, workforce-coordination, public-transit-reliability

## Scope

**ADMINISTRATIVE / LOGISTICS SCHEDULING coordination only.** This
practice never operates a switch or signal. It coordinates the
administrative and logistics work around railway brake, signal and
switch operators — inspection/status logging, crew scheduling,
safety-concern escalation and maintenance procurement coordination —
never the physical switch-throw, signal-clearance or track-routing
decision itself.

## Customer

- rail infrastructure operators
- rail yard and signal-box operations teams

## Offer

- switch/signal inspection and service-record logging
- operator-roster and shift scheduling coordination
- safety-concern intake and human-escalation routing
- signal/switch equipment maintenance procurement coordination

## Revenue

- monthly operations-coordination retainer
- per-maintenance-order coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no proposal may ever finalize a switch-throw, signal-clearance or
  track-routing decision — this is a hard, permanent block with no
  approval path, at every maturity level
- operator/section provenance required before any coordination action
- any surfaced safety concern always requires human sign-off
- maintenance-order procurement above the section's registered cost
  ceiling always requires human sign-off
- coordination and audit records are auditable, not editable
